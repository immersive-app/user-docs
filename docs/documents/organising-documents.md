# Organising Documents with Folders

Organize your documents in Immersive with folders and subfolders to keep your materials structured, streamline study sessions, and access curated content for effective language learning.

## Creating Folders

Folders help you organize your documents for efficient access and study.

To create a folder:
1. Navigate to the **Documents** section in Immersive.
2. Click on the **"New Folder"** button.
3. Provide a name for the folder and confirm.

You can also create subfolders by navigating into an existing folder and repeating the steps above.

---

## Renaming Folders

To rename an existing folder:
1. Locate the folder you want to rename in the **Documents** section.
2. Click the **Options (⋮)** icon next to the folder name.
3. Select **"Rename Folder"** from the dropdown menu.
4. Enter the new folder name and save your changes.

Renaming helps keep your folder structure clear and relevant.

---

## Moving Documents Between Folders

You can rearrange your documents by moving them between folders.

To move a document:
1. Find the document in its current folder or the main **Documents** section.
2. Click the **Options (⋮)** icon next to the document name.
3. Select **"Move to Folder"** and choose the destination folder from the list.
4. Confirm the move.

This allows you to keep related documents together for easier access.

---

## Deleting Folders

When a folder is deleted, all its contents—including subfolders and documents—will also be removed. Immersive will display a warning dialog to confirm your action.

To delete a folder:
1. Locate the folder in the **Documents** section.
2. Click the **Options (⋮)** icon next to the folder name.
3. Select **"Delete Folder"** from the dropdown menu.
4. Review the confirmation dialog and click **"Delete"** to proceed.

Be cautious when deleting folders to avoid losing important materials.

---

## Accessing Curated Content

Immersive offers curated documents from sources like Wikipedia, organized into folders for easy exploration.

To access curated content:
1. Go to the **Curated Documents** section in Immersive.
2. Browse the available folders to find topics that interest you.
3. Open a document to start reading and learning.

Curated content provides real-world materials to enhance your language studies.

---

By Organising your documents into folders and subfolders, you can keep your study materials structured and make the most of your learning time.
