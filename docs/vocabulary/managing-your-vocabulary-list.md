# Managing Your Vocabulary List

Learn how to manage your vocabulary effectively in Immersive to track your progress, organize your learning, and make the most out of your study sessions.

## Learning Phases

Immersive organizes your Vocabulary into **Learning Phases** to help you focus on mastering each item at the right time. Learning Phases are tied to the Vocabulary item, not individual cards. This means that the same Vocabulary item will share the same Learning Phase across all cards.

The Learning Phases are:

- **New**: The Vocabulary item has not been reviewed yet.
- **Learning**: The item is in the process of being learned but is not yet retained in long-term memory.
- **Review**: The item has been learned and is being reviewed periodically for retention.
- **Relearning**: The item was previously learned but has been forgotten and needs to be relearned.

These phases ensure your study sessions are tailored to your progress and retention needs.

## Filtering and Sorting

You can customize your view of the Vocabulary List to focus on what you need most.

### Access your Vocabulary List
To access your Vocabulary List, navigate to the Vocabulary section in Immersive.

### Filter your Vocabulary List
- Filter by **Learning Phase** to focus on items in New, Learning, Review, or Relearning phases.
- Filter by **Verbs**, **Words**, or **Sentences**.
- Search by any text to quickly find specific items.

### Sort your Vocabulary List
Click on the column headers to sort items alphabetically or by their Learning Phase.

These tools make it easy to organize and focus your study sessions.

---

## Creating a Filtered Deck

Filtered Decks allow you to create a study set based on your filtered Vocabulary List. Follow these steps:

1. Filter the Vocabulary Items using the options described above.
2. Click **"Create Deck"**.
3. Provide a name for the new Deck.

Once created, you can use this Deck for focused study sessions.

## Deleting Vocabulary

When you delete Vocabulary, cards containing those items will be deleted from any Decks. To delete 1 or multiple entries, follow these steps:

1. Filter the Vocabulary Items using the options described above.
2. The **Delete Vocabulary** button will appear
3. Press the **Delete** button and the confirmation message.

---

## Playing Audio

To hear the pronunciation of a Vocabulary item:

1. Click the audio player icon next to the item.
2. If the audio hasn’t been created yet, Immersive will generate it on-demand. There may be a slight delay during this process.

Listening to audio helps you practice pronunciation and reinforce your learning.

By effectively managing your Vocabulary List, you can track progress, stay organized, and tailor your learning to your specific goals.
