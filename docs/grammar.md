**Grammar Notes** provide a structured reference to guide your language learning journey. Organized by proficiency levels and enriched with examples, translations, and audio pronunciations, Grammar Notes offer a comprehensive way to master grammar concepts.

### About Grammar Notes in Immersive

Grammar is the backbone of any language, and mastering it is key to achieving fluency. Immersive’s Grammar Notes serve as a clear and detailed reference, enabling you to build confidence and competence in the language you’re learning.

Key features include:

- **Graded Concepts:** Grammar Notes are organized by CEFR levels (A1 to C2), helping you focus on material relevant to your skill level.
- **Rich Examples:** Each note includes example words and sentences with translations and audio pronunciations, bringing grammar to life.
- **Progress Tracking:** Mark off notes as you learn them to track your progress and identify areas for review.
- **Bilingual Display:** View Grammar Notes in English or, for advanced learners, in the target language to deepen immersion.

Whether you’re tackling basic sentence structure or refining complex syntax, Grammar Notes keep your learning organized and effective.

---

### How Grammar Notes Work

Grammar Notes are designed to provide clarity and context for learners at every stage. Here’s how they function:

#### Graded Learning
- **CEFR Alignment:** Notes are divided into levels from A1 (beginner) to C2 (proficient), ensuring a smooth progression.
- **Tailored Focus:** Start with foundational grammar at lower levels, then explore advanced nuances as you progress.

#### Comprehensive Examples
- **Example Sentences:** Grammar concepts are illustrated with real-world examples, offering clear context for usage.
- **Translations and Pronunciations:** Hear example sentences read aloud by AI-generated native speakers, and see their translations for better understanding.

#### Progress Tracking
- **Mark as Known:** Once you’ve mastered a Grammar Note, mark it as known to keep track of your progress.
- **Personalized Learning Path:** Focus on unmarked notes to ensure steady progress without revisiting what you’ve already learned.

#### Language Display Options
- **Bilingual Support:** For beginners, notes are displayed in English. For advanced learners, switch to the target language to enhance immersion.

### Using Grammar Notes Effectively

Grammar Notes are an invaluable resource when used alongside other Immersive tools:

1. **Reference While Reading or Writing:**  
   Use Grammar Notes to clarify rules and structures when reading documents or writing your own text.

2. **Integrate with Vocabulary and Flashcards:**  
   Pair grammar concepts with vocabulary lists or flashcards to reinforce learning in context.

3. **Review Regularly:**  
   Return to marked notes periodically to ensure retention and fill in any gaps.

4. **Combine with Practice:**  
   Apply grammar rules actively in writing and speaking exercises to solidify your understanding.


More information can be found in [Review Grammar](/docs/grammar/review-grammar)

### Where to Start

Getting started with Grammar Notes is simple:

1. **Select Your Level:**  
   Begin with A1-level notes if you’re new to the language, or choose a higher level to match your proficiency.

2. **Explore Topics:**  
   Browse Grammar Notes by category, such as sentence structure, verb conjugation, or prepositions.

3. **Mark Your Progress:**  
   Check off notes as you master them, and revisit unmarked notes to continue building your skills.

4. **Engage with Examples:**  
   Read, listen to, and translate example sentences to deepen your understanding of each concept.
