# Flashcards

**Flashcards** are a powerful tool to help you master vocabulary, sentences, and verbs through targeted, efficient practice. Designed with advanced spaced repetition and interactive modes, Flashcards help you commit language concepts to long-term memory while refining your skills across reading, writing, listening, and speaking.

### About Flashcards in Immersive

Flashcards are a cornerstone of effective language learning, offering a structured way to review and reinforce what you’ve learned. Immersive’s Flashcards integrate seamlessly with your Vocabulary List, enabling you to focus on words, sentences, and verbs most relevant to your learning journey.

Key features include:

- **Vocabulary Integration:** Automatically create cards from your Vocabulary List, including words, sentences, and verb conjugations.
- **Spaced Repetition:** Powered by the FSRS algorithm, Immersive optimizes review intervals to maximize memory retention.
- **Interactive Modes:** Choose from four distinct modes to practice reading, writing, listening, and speaking.

With Immersive, Flashcards become more than just memorization—they’re an engaging, versatile way to develop your language skills.

---

### How Flashcards Work

Immersive’s Flashcards are designed to suit a variety of learning preferences and skill levels. Here’s how they function:

#### Integration with Vocabulary
- **Vocabulary-Driven:** Flashcards are automatically generated from your Vocabulary List as you interact with documents and other features.
- **Customizable Content:** Modify cards to suit your needs by adding or removing example sentences, or changing prompts.

#### Spaced Repetition
- **Powered by FSRS:** Immersive uses the advanced FSRS algorithm to calculate the ideal review interval for each card, helping you focus on what matters most.

#### Practice Modes
Each Flashcard can be practiced in one of four interactive modes:

1. **Read Mode**  
   - Review the card content and evaluate your recall using buttons (similar to Anki’s system).
   - Self-assess your answer to decide if you need more review or are ready to move on.

2. **Write Mode**  
   - Complete Cloze deletions or type your answers.
   - Focuses on active recall and reinforces spelling and grammar.

3. **Listen Mode**  
   - Hear the content read aloud with AI-generated native pronunciation.
   - Answer based on what you hear, improving your listening skills.

4. **Speak Mode**  
   - Practice speaking the card’s content, and let Immersive evaluate your pronunciation.
   - Receive feedback to refine your accent and fluency.

### Using Flashcards in Practice

Flashcards in Immersive integrate seamlessly with your overall learning workflow:

- **From Vocabulary to Flashcards**  
  Every word, sentence, or verb you add to your Vocabulary List can automatically appear in your Flashcard reviews.

- **Personalized Learning**  
  Immersive tracks your progress with each card and adapts the review schedule to your learning pace, ensuring efficient use of your time.

- **Comprehensive Practice**  
  Rotate through all four practice modes to strengthen different aspects of language learning, from comprehension to speaking fluency.

### Where to Start

Getting started with Flashcards in Immersive is simple:

1. **Build Your Vocabulary List**  
   Add words, sentences, or verbs by clicking on them in Documents or other Immersive tools.

2. **Start a Flashcard Session**  
   Open the Flashcards feature and select the mode you want to practice (Read, Write, Listen, or Speak).

3. **Review and Track Progress**  
   Use the FSRS algorithm to guide your reviews, focusing on the content that needs the most reinforcement.

4. **Switch Modes**  
   Experiment with different modes to practice a variety of skills, ensuring well-rounded learning.
