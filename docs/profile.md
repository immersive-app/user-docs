# Profile & Progress

Your Profile is the command center for everything about you as a learner: where you stand, what you have done, the words you have collected, the people you study with, and your account itself. Open **Profile** from the navigation — it remembers which tab you used last.

---

## Progress

**Where you stand.** Immersive tracks **comprehension** and **production** separately, because understanding legitimately runs ahead of speaking and writing — that gap is the method working, not a problem. For each skill — Reading, Listening, Writing, Speaking — you see your level alongside the evidence behind it: documents and words read, listening reviews, graded writing submissions, peer evaluations.

**By domain.** Rollups for each area — your decks (cards mastered, learning, new, and due now), your documents, your vocabulary, and your graded writing. Each row opens its surface.

**Up next.** What is actually waiting: due cards to review, words from this week not yet in a deck. When nothing is waiting, it says so.

New accounts see **First steps** instead: your starting level from onboarding and two concrete actions — open your first deck, and read something at your level.

## Activity

Your study, added up — effort only, never judgment:

- A **heatmap** of the last 12 months: one square per day, darker meaning more study, with your total study days, longest run, and current run.
- The **study log**: your sessions and reviews, day by day, with a full log available.

## Vocabulary

The words and sentences you have collected while studying — searchable, filterable, and one action away from becoming a deck. See [Vocabulary](vocabulary.md).

## Evaluate

After studying with a classmate, you can trade quick evaluations — one overall impression (Excellent, Good, or Getting there) plus an optional comment. It is a courtesy between learners, never a ranking.

- **Show my code** displays a short code for your classmate to enter. Codes last 10 minutes and work once.
- **Enter a code** from a classmate's screen to evaluate them: pick an impression, add a sentence if you like, and send.

Evaluations you give and receive are listed on the tab, and spoken evaluations count as evidence on your Progress tab.

## Teachers

If you study with a teacher, you can let them follow your progress — on your terms:

1. Choose **Create access code** and pick exactly what it unlocks: Progress, Activity, and/or Vocabulary.
2. Set an expiry (a week, a month, end of term, or none) and hand the code to your teacher.
3. They claim it and see the parts you chose — read-only, never your account details.

**Revoke** any teacher's access at any time; it ends immediately. Unclaimed codes can be cancelled.

## Account

Everything administrative lives here:

- **Profile** — name, profile picture, location, and gender.
- **Languages** — the same grid as onboarding: what you speak, what you are learning, your level, and your display language. Change it any time.
- **Email and password** — updating either asks for your current password.
- **Your data** — **Download your personal data** emails you everything you have added, as a file, within a day.
- **Remove my account** — deletes your account and all its data. This cannot be undone.
