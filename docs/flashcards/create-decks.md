### Creating Flashcard Decks

Immersive makes it easy to create custom flashcard decks to focus your learning and practice effectively. Whether you're working with numbers, verbs, or vocabulary, Immersive provides flexible options for building decks tailored to your needs.

## Creating Number Decks

Number decks are ideal for practicing numbers in your target language. Here's how to create one:

1. Navigate to the **Flashcards** section in Immersive and select **"Create Deck"**.
2. Choose **"Number Deck"**.
3. Specify a range of numbers (e.g., 1–100) or select predefined number sets (e.g., decimals, fractions).
4. Confirm to generate flashcards with numbers translated and pronounced in your target language.

With number decks, you can practice recognizing and pronouncing numbers fluently.

---

## Creating Verb Conjugation Decks

Verb conjugation decks help you master verbs and their various forms with contextual examples. Follow these steps:

1. Go to the **Flashcards** section and click **"Create Deck"**.
2. Select **"Verb Conjugation Deck"**.
3. Choose:
   - A list of infinitive verbs.
   - The tenses you want to practice (e.g., present, past, future).
4. Immersive will:
   - Automatically generate flashcards for each verb and tense.
   - Associate example sentences with each conjugation, drawn from the Tatoeba project.

These decks provide an efficient way to practice verb forms with real-world context.

---

## Creating Vocabulary Decks

Vocabulary decks are perfect for practicing words and sentences relevant to your learning goals. Here's how to create one:

1. In the **Flashcards** section, click **"Create Deck"** and choose **"Vocabulary Deck"**.
2. Add your content:
   - **Words**: Type or paste a list of words.
     - Optionally, enable **"Associate Tatoeba Sentences"** to include example sentences for each word.
   - **Sentences**: Add full sentences for contextual practice.
3. Immersive will:
   - Generate flashcards with translations, pronunciations, and example sentences.

Vocabulary decks allow you to focus on key phrases and words, tailored to your interests.

---

## Customizing Decks

After creating a deck, you can further personalize it:

- **Add or Remove Cards**: Modify the deck to suit your current learning focus.
- **Edit Flashcards**: Adjust translations or examples directly within each card.
- **Rename Decks**: Keep your decks organized by renaming them as needed.

---

By creating custom flashcard decks, you can tailor your practice to your unique learning needs, ensuring that you make steady progress toward fluency.
