### Managing Decks

Immersive makes it simple to organize, view, and modify your Flashcard Decks, helping you stay on top of your language learning progress.

## Sorting Decks

All your decks are sorted alphabetically in Immersive, making it easy to find the one you need. Whether you’re studying vocabulary, verbs, or specific topics, you’ll always have quick access to your deck of choice.

---

## Viewing Deck Details

The **Deck Index View** provides an at-a-glance overview of each deck's status, helping you track your learning progress.

### Columns in the Deck Index View:
- **Deck Name**: The title of your deck.
- **New**: The number of flashcards in the deck that you haven’t reviewed yet.
- **Learn**: Flashcards that are in the process of being learned but aren’t retained in long-term memory.
- **Due**: Flashcards that are ready for review based on the spaced repetition schedule.

This view helps you prioritize your study sessions and focus on decks requiring attention.

---

## Renaming a Deck

You can rename any deck to better reflect its content or purpose.

### Steps to rename a deck:
1. Navigate to the **Deck Index View**.
2. Click the **Edit** icon next to the deck name.
3. Enter a new name for the deck.
4. Save your changes.

Renaming a deck is a helpful way to stay organized as your learning evolves.

---

## Deleting a Deck

If a deck is no longer needed, you can delete it to declutter your learning environment.

### Steps to delete a deck:
1. Navigate to the **Deck Index View**.
2. Click the **Delete** icon next to the deck you want to remove.
3. Confirm the deletion in the dialog box.

> **Note**: Deleting a deck will remove all its flashcards and associated progress. Be sure to double-check before confirming.

---

By effectively managing your decks, you can stay organized, monitor your progress, and focus your study sessions on what matters most.
