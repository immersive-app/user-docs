### Reviewing Flashcards

Immersive’s Flashcards offer four interactive review modes designed to reinforce your language learning through reading, writing, listening, and speaking. Each mode targets a specific skill, ensuring a well-rounded practice experience.

## Read Mode

Read Mode helps you review flashcards and self-assess your recall using interactive buttons. It’s similar to Anki’s review system and ensures efficient spaced repetition.

### How it works:
1. **Review Content**: Read the word, sentence, or verb conjugation displayed on the card.
2. **Evaluate Recall**: Use one of the four buttons to assess how well you remembered the content:
   - **Again**: For items you couldn’t recall, so they reappear sooner.
   - **Hard**: For items you found difficult but remembered.
   - **Good**: For items you recalled with some effort.
   - **Easy**: For items you remembered effortlessly, allowing for longer intervals before review.
3. Immersive will adjust when the card is shown next based on your input, optimizing your learning progress.

---

## Write Mode

Write Mode strengthens active recall by having you complete Cloze deletions or type full answers, reinforcing spelling and grammar.

### How it works:
1. **Cloze Deletions**: Fill in the blanks within sentences using:
   - A list of shuffled letters as hints.
   - A freeform keyboard for more advanced practice.
2. **Immediate Feedback**: See corrections instantly, helping you learn from your mistakes.

Write Mode is perfect for learners who want to focus on writing skills and grammar mastery.

---

## Listen Mode

Listen Mode sharpens your listening skills by having you type out what you hear, improving your comprehension and spelling.

### How it works:
1. **Hear Content**: Immersive plays the card content aloud using AI-generated native pronunciation.
2. **Type Your Answer**: Write down what you heard.
3. **Check Accuracy**: Compare your answer to the correct response to assess your comprehension.

This mode is ideal for focusing on auditory learning and honing your ability to understand spoken language.

---

## Speak Mode

Speak Mode develops your speaking skills by evaluating your pronunciation and fluency.

### How it works:
1. **Practice Speaking**: Say the word, sentence, or phrase displayed on the card.
2. **Receive Pronunciation Scores**: Immersive evaluates your speech and provides a score along with detailed feedback.
3. **Refine Your Accent**: Use the feedback to improve your pronunciation and fluency.

Speak Mode is invaluable for practicing real-world speaking scenarios and gaining confidence in your spoken language skills.

---

By practicing flashcards in these four modes, Immersive ensures that you’re engaging with your vocabulary in diverse and effective ways. Whether you’re reviewing, writing, listening, or speaking, every mode contributes to building a strong foundation in your target language.
