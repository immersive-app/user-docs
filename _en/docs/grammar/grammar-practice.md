# Grammar practice

Reading a note once is not enough to make a grammar point stick. **Practice** turns a note's exercises into review cards that come back on the same memory-based schedule as your decks.

## Practising a note

On a grammar note, **Practice** (with how many cards are due, new, or waiting) creates the note's cards, if you have none yet, and starts a review of that note. The cards go into a deck called **Grammar practice** for the language, which you also see in My Decks.

## Reviewing grammar

Grammar reviews can cover one note, one area, one level, or everything:

- On the Grammar page: **Review 3 due**, or **Practice · 5 new** when nothing is due.
- On a level page: **Review 3 due at B1**.
- On an area page: **Review this area** or **Practice this area**.
- On a note: **Practice**.

The review works like a deck review (see [Reviewing cards](../decks/reviewing-cards.md)), with three modes:

- **Read**: read the card, recall the answer, and grade yourself (**Grade it to continue**).
- **Write**: type the answer; Immersive checks it and proposes a grade.
- **Choose**: pick the right form from the options (number keys work too); choosing submits and grades the card.

Immersive remembers the mode you use for grammar separately from your deck reviews. A card that cannot be answered in the chosen mode is shown in the nearest one that fits.

## When there is nothing to review

With no grammar cards yet, the review page says **No cards yet** and suggests notes to try. When cards exist but none are due, it says when the next one comes due. Either way, it offers the recommended notes, **Browse** your level, and **Back to Grammar**.

## Your standing

Every review updates your mastery of the note, its area, and its level, shown on the Grammar page and on each note. Due grammar cards also appear under **Up next** on [Profile → Progress](../profile.md#progress).
