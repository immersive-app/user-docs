# Grammar practice

Reading a note once is not enough to make a grammar point stick. Practising turns notes' exercises into review cards that come back on the same memory-based schedule as your decks.

## Practising

You practise a level or an area at a time:

- On a level page, **Practise all · 12 cards** at the head of the list of areas covers every note in the level, and **Practise** on an area's row covers that area.
- On an area page, **Practise all · 5 cards** at the head of the list of notes covers every note in the area.

Each button makes the cards you do not have yet and starts the review straight away. The cards go into a deck called **Grammar practice** for the language, which you also see in My Decks. A note with no exercises is practised from its own example sentences. A level or area with nothing to practise has no button.

On a note itself, each exercise you answer becomes a card in Grammar practice too, and every answer counts as a review of it: a right answer as **Good**, a wrong one as **Again**, whether the card was due or not. Another try after a wrong answer is one more review. The same answer sent twice in a row, by a double tap, counts once. See [Reading grammar notes](review-grammar.md#exercises).

## Reviewing grammar

Grammar reviews can cover one note, one area, one level, or everything:

- On the Grammar page, on a wider screen: **Review 3 due**, or **Practice · 5 new** when nothing is due.
- On a level page: **Review 3 due at B1**.
- On an area page: **Review this area · 3 due**, or **Practise this area**.
- On a note: on a phone, when a note has exercises due, **Continue** on the Grammar page opens it on those exercises. Once they are answered, the note says **Reviewed for today.**, and **Next note** goes on to the next one.

The review works like a deck review (see [Reviewing cards](../decks/reviewing-cards.md)), with three modes:

- **Read**: read the card, recall the answer, and grade yourself (**Grade it to continue**).
- **Write**: type the answer; Immersive checks it and proposes a grade.
- **Choose**: pick the right form from the options (number keys work too); choosing submits and grades the card.

Immersive remembers the mode you use for grammar separately from your deck reviews. A card that cannot be answered in the chosen mode is shown in the nearest one that fits.

## When there is nothing to review

With no grammar cards yet, the review page says **No cards yet** and suggests notes to try. When cards exist but none are due, it says when the next one comes due. Either way, it offers the recommended notes, **Browse** your level, and **Back to Grammar**.

## Test yourself

A quiz checks a whole level or area at once. **Test yourself** is on a level page and on an area page, under its main button, when there is something to ask. In the grammar map (**By level and area** on the Grammar page on a wider screen, **Grammar by level** on [Profile → Home](../profile.md#home)), each level has **Test yourself on B1** too: the button with a tick beside the level's mastery, or, on a phone, the link beside **Open B1** once you open the level.

A quiz is up to ten questions on one page. They are your own grammar cards for the level's or area's notes, one note per question while there are notes enough, the kinds mixed. For a note you have no cards for yet, the quiz first makes them in **Grammar practice**, as **Practise** does. You pick an option to answer a choice question and type the others. There is no hint and no coach while you are being tested.

Answer them all, then choose **Check all**. The page then shows:

- your score ("7 of 10 right"), and **Test again**, which draws a new quiz;
- **By note**: each note the quiz asked about, how many of its questions you got right ("1 of 2"), and your mastery of it now; the title opens the note;
- each question as you answered it: **Correct**, or **Not right** or **Not answered** with the right answer ("Answer: …").

Every answer counts as a review of its card, graded as a grammar review grades it, and a question left empty counts as wrong. So a quiz moves your mastery and the cards' schedule like any review, and the cards come back in your grammar reviews. A quiz is checked once: sending it a second time changes nothing.

## Your standing

Every review updates your mastery of the note, its area, and its level, shown on the Grammar page and on each note. On [Profile → Home](../profile.md#home), **Grammar by level** shows each level's mastery and how many of its grammar cards are due.
