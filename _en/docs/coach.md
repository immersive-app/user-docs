# Coach

The coach answers questions about what you are studying. Open it from the thing you have a question about, and it starts with that in view: a review card, a grammar note, a verb, a word, a text, or a correction.

## Asking a question

A round coach button floats at the bottom right of the screen, in a browser and in the iOS app alike; on a grammar note it sits just above the note's settings button (beside it on a computer). The reader and review sessions place their own. The button's name, which a computer shows when you point at it and a screen reader reads, says what the coach will see:

- **Ask the coach**: a general question, from any page (and, on a computer, from the top of your Profile).
- **Ask about this card**: in a review, once the answer is shown: at the top of the card on a phone, at its bottom right on a wider screen.
- **Ask about this note**, or **Ask about this exercise** on an exercise: on a grammar note. Selecting a passage in a note offers to ask about just that passage.
- **Ask about this verb**: on a verb page or a form page.
- **Ask about this comparison**: on a grammar-of-reading comparison.
- **Ask about this text**: the chat button at the bottom right of the reader and the editor, and on a correction page.
- **Ask about this**: in a word's or sentence's **More** panel in the reader.
- **Ask about this correction**: on each correction of your writing.

Type your question and press Enter (Shift+Enter starts a new line). The answer appears as it is written. On a computer, an empty chat offers a few **Suggested** questions, such as "Break this sentence down" in the reader or "Compare with a language I know" on a grammar note; **Expand** and **Shrink** change the panel's size.

## How the coach answers

- In your display language, unless you write to it in the language you are learning.
- Briefly, and about language: it points out where a language you know works the same way, and it declines questions that have nothing to do with language learning.
- Each time you open the coach, it starts a new chat.
- One message at a time: while the answer is being written, the message box waits until it ends.

If you send many messages in a row, the coach asks you to wait a minute ("Too many messages in a row"). If it cannot answer, your message is kept and **Try again** sends it again.

## Practising with the coach

On a grammar note, the coach offers two more ways in, above the message box:

- **Practise this note**, or **Practise this exercise** on an exercise, starts a practice chat in the language you are learning that calls for the note's grammar. **Show hint** under a turn shows its hint.
- **Talk about this note** starts a free conversation in the language you are learning, steered by the note's grammar. Each mistake you make is corrected under your message (**Correction:**).

## Your chats

The coach keeps your chats, and its list is in the coach itself:

- On a phone, the chats button at the top of the coach (**All chats**) opens the list. In the iOS app the list opens as a screen of its own.
- On a computer, **Expand** makes the panel the full height of the screen, and **Full screen** then shows the list beside the chat. **Shrink** and **Exit full screen** step back.

**Chats** lists them newest first, grouped by day, each with its first line, what it was about (Card, Note, Verb, Text...), and the time; **Show earlier chats** loads older ones. Tap one to read it again and carry on; its label links back to what it was about. If that item has since been deleted, the chat keeps what it saw, and the list marks it **deleted**. **New chat**, at the top of the list, starts a general chat. With no chats yet, the list says **No chats yet**.

The bin button deletes a chat, after a confirmation: the coach will not remember it.

## The Coach button in the editor

When you are writing, **Coach** in the editor's writing assistant is different: it gives a one-off review of your saved text, not a chat. See [Writing documents](documents/writing-documents.md#the-coach-review).
