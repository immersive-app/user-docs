# Coach

The coach answers questions about what you are studying. Open it from the thing you have a question about, and it starts with that in view: a review card, a grammar note, a verb, a word, a text, or a correction.

## Asking a question

Pages with the navigation bar have a coach button at the bottom of the screen; the reader and review sessions have their own. The label says what the coach will see:

- **Ask the coach**: a general question, from any page (and from the top of your Profile).
- **Ask about this card**: in a review, once the answer is shown.
- **Ask about this note**, or **Ask about this exercise** on an exercise: on a grammar note. Selecting a passage in a note offers to ask about just that passage.
- **Ask about this verb**: on a verb page or a form page.
- **Ask about this comparison**: on a grammar-of-reading comparison.
- **Ask about this text**: in the reader (also in the reading controls), and on a correction page.
- **Ask about this**: in a word's or sentence's **More** panel in the reader.
- **Ask about this correction**: on each correction of your writing.

Type your question and press Enter (Shift+Enter starts a new line). The answer appears as it is written. On a computer, an empty chat offers a few **Suggested** questions, such as "Break this sentence down" in the reader or "Compare with a language I know" on a grammar note; **Expand** and **Shrink** change the panel's size.

## How the coach answers

- In your display language, unless you write to it in the language you are learning.
- Briefly, and about language: it points out where a language you know works the same way, and it declines questions that have nothing to do with language learning.
- Each time you open the coach, it starts a new chat.

If you send many messages in a row, the coach asks you to wait a minute ("Too many messages in a row"). If it cannot answer, your message is kept and **Try again** sends it again.

## Your chats

**Profile → Coach** lists your chats, newest first, each with what it was about (Card, Note, Verb, Text...), its first line, and its date. Tap one to read it again; its label links back to what it was about. If that item has since been deleted, the chat keeps what it saw.

The bin button deletes a chat, after a confirmation: the coach will not remember it.

Your chats are private: teachers you give access to cannot see them.

## The Coach button in the editor

When you are writing, **Coach** in the editor's toolbar is different: it gives a one-off review of your saved text, not a chat. See [Writing documents](documents/writing-documents.md#the-coach-review).
