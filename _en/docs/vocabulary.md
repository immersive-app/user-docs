# Vocabulary

Your Vocabulary is the list of words and sentences you have looked up while studying. You never type anything into it: it collects itself as you use Immersive, and it lives in your Profile, under the **Vocabulary** tab. The words you tap and the entries you save are also cards in a deck, **My vocabulary**, so they come back for review.

## How vocabulary is collected

- **Tapping a word.** Tapping a word in the language you are learning to see its translation (in the reader, a grammar note, a verb page's example sentences, a comparison) records it and makes it a card in your My vocabulary deck at once, with the sentence you tapped it in. Taps on a review card or in an exercise are part of answering, and add nothing.
- **Tapping a sentence** records it, without making a card.
- **Saving it.** **Save to vocabulary** in a word's or a sentence's **More** panel records it and marks it as saved, which makes it a card too.
- **Reviewing it.** A word or sentence card reviewed in a deck counts too: **Again** or **Hard** as a miss, **Good** or **Easy** as a review.

Each entry remembers how often you have seen, missed, and reviewed it, so the list doubles as a record of what you keep looking up.

## My vocabulary

My vocabulary is a vocabulary deck like any other, one for each language you learn, listed in My Decks. A word becomes a word card with its sentence as the example; a saved sentence becomes a sentence card. A word that is already a card is not added twice.

When a tap has just made a word a card, its **More** panel says **Added**, with **Undo** to take it back out. A word you take out, dismiss, or delete is not added again by a tap; only **Save** brings it back.

## Browsing your list

Open **Profile → Vocabulary**. At the top, the My vocabulary deck shows its cards and how many are due, with **Study** to review them.

The list shows the language you are learning now, most recent first; a line counts how many entries you have collected and how many you have saved. You can:

- **Search your words** with the search box.
- **Filter by type**: all types, words only, or sentences only.
- Tick **Saved only** to see just the entries you have saved.

Each row shows the word or sentence (tap it for its translation and audio) with its translation, the sentence a word was saved in, with the word marked, then where you met it and its counts (seen, missed, reviewed). A filled bookmark marks an entry that is saved, and so a card.

The list shows the 50 most recent matching entries ("Showing 50 of 212"). To find an older one, search or filter.

## Saving and dismissing

Tick the entries you care about (or **Select all**), then:

- **Save** marks them as saved and makes each a card in My vocabulary. A dismissed entry comes back with its review history.
- **Dismiss** takes them out of review: their cards are suspended, and the rows stay in the list, dimmed, marked **Suspended**.

## If your list is empty

Vocabulary only fills as you study. The fastest way to start it is **Open a document** and read: every word you look up appears here. See [Reading documents](documents/reading-documents.md).
