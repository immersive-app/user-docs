# Your account

Creating an account is covered in [Getting started](../index.md#creating-an-account). This page covers everything after that. Your settings live in **Profile → Account**; see [Profile & Progress](profile.md#account).

## Signing in

**Log In** asks for your email and password. **Remember me** keeps you signed in on that device.

Signing out: open your profile picture in the navigation bar and choose **Log out**.

## Can't log in?

**Can't log in?** on the sign-in page offers three ways back in:

- **Forgot password?** emails you a link to choose a new password. The link works for six hours.
- **Unlock my account**: after three wrong passwords in a row, your account locks, and this emails you an unlock link.
- **Resend account confirmation**: for when the confirmation email from sign-up went missing.

## Changing your password

In **Profile → Account → Password**, enter your current password and the new one twice, then **Update password**. Your email address is shown there but cannot be changed in the app.

## Invitations

Signed in, **Invite a friend** in the footer of Immersive's public pages sends an invitation by email: enter your friend's first name, last name, and email, and choose **Send an invitation**. Your friend follows the link in the email, chooses the language they speak and the one they want to learn, and sets a password.

## Emails from Immersive

- **Account emails**: confirming your address, resetting your password, unlocking your account.
- **The daily review email**, when your decks have cards waiting. See [Navigation](dashboard.md#the-daily-review-email).
- **Anki exports** you ask for, with the deck file attached. See [Managing decks](decks/managing-decks.md#exporting-to-anki).
- **Your personal data**, when you ask for it (below).

## Your data

**Download your personal data** in **Profile → Account** asks Immersive to prepare a copy of your data. Immersive emails you a link to it within a day. Asking again while one is on its way just tells you it is coming.

## Deleting your account

**Remove my account** in **Profile → Account** deletes your account and all its data, after a confirmation. This cannot be undone. You are signed out straight away.
