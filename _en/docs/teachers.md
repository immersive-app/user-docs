# Teachers

If you study with a teacher, you can let them follow your progress, on your terms: you choose what they see and for how long, and you can end it at any time. They see those parts of your profile read-only, and never your account details.

## Giving a teacher access

1. Open **Profile → Teachers** and choose **Create access code**.
2. Pick what the code unlocks: **Progress** (levels and domain rollups), **Activity** (heatmap, effort, and the study log), and **Vocabulary** (collected words and their counts). Progress and Activity are ticked to start with.
3. Pick when access **Expires**: **End of term** (31 August), **In one month**, **In one week**, or **No expiry**.
4. Choose **Create code** and hand the code to your teacher, or use **Copy link** to send them a link with the code filled in.

A code that nobody claims lapses after seven days. Until it is claimed, it is listed under **Access codes** with **Cancel**.

## Ending access

Teachers who claimed a code are listed with what they can see and since when. **Revoke** ends a teacher's access immediately.

## For teachers

To follow a student you need an Immersive account of your own:

1. Sign in, then open the link your student sent you, or go to **Claim an access code** (`/teach/claim`) and type the code.
2. Choose **Claim code**.

You then see the parts of your student's profile they chose, under a banner that says whose profile it is, that it is read-only, and when access expires:

- **Progress**: where they stand, and their decks, documents, and writing.
- **Activity**: their effort, study days, and study log.
- **Vocabulary**: the words and sentences they collected, with a type filter.

Immersive has no menu entry for the students you follow, so bookmark the page to come back to it. If a code is not accepted, the page says why: it does not match, it is your own, or it is no longer valid (ask your student for a fresh one).
