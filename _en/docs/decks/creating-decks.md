# Creating decks

Building a deck takes two steps: create the deck, then add cards to it. A deck reviews nothing until it has cards, so the workspace takes you straight from one to the other.

## To create a deck

1. Open **Decks** and choose the **+** button (**Create a new deck**).
2. Give the deck a title in the language you are learning and one in your own language. Both are required: reviews can show either side.
3. Optionally pick a **Folder** (or type a new folder name) and add **Tags**. Leaving the folder blank keeps the deck at the top level of My Decks; tags group decks across folders.
4. Choose **Save and add cards**.

## Adding cards

The deck's workspace has four card types (**Word**, **Sentence**, **Verb**, **Number**) and two other sources: **Upload spreadsheet** and **From bookmarks**.

### Words

Type a **Word** and, optionally, its **Translation**. Leave the translation blank and Immersive fills it in. Pressing Enter adds the card and clears the field for the next word, so you can enter a list quickly.

### Sentences

Enter sentences one per line and choose **Add cards**. Translations fill in automatically; you can edit them in the card list afterwards.

### Verbs

1. Pick verbs from the **Essential**, **Common**, or **All** list. Essential holds the first verbs a new learner needs; Common and All have a search box. Immersive remembers the list you used last.
2. Tick the **Tenses** you want to practise.
3. Choose a card style: **One card per person** (each person reviewed separately) or **All persons on one card** (the whole tense table on a single card).
4. Choose **Create cards**.

### Numbers

Enter numbers separated by commas, without spaces, for example `17,70,71,80`, and choose **Add cards**. Each number becomes a card that you read, write, hear, or say in words.

## From a spreadsheet

Choose **Upload spreadsheet** to import many cards at once:

1. Choose a **CSV or XLSX** file with two columns: the text in the language you are learning, and its translation. The translation is optional. **Download template.csv** gives you a file to start from. Up to 500 rows are read.
2. Choose **Preview**. Each row becomes a word card or a sentence card, depending on its text. Rows already in the deck are marked **In Deck** and arrive unticked, so you do not create duplicates; translations Immersive filled in are marked **Auto** so you can glance over them.
3. Tick the rows you want and choose **Import cards**.

## From your vocabulary

**From bookmarks** lists the words and sentences you marked as saved in your [Vocabulary](../vocabulary.md), for this deck's languages, all ticked. Filter by **All**, **Words**, or **Sentences**, untick what you do not want, and choose **Add cards**. The entries stay saved in your Vocabulary.

## Example sentences

A word card or a verb card can carry an example sentence. In the card list, choose **Add example** (or **Change example**) on the card, then:

- **From your documents**: sentences you have read that contain the word appear first.
- **Write your own**: type a sentence and choose **Attach sentence**.
- **Generate**: Immersive writes one sentence at your level; choose **Regenerate** if it does not fit.

## Other ways decks get made

You do not always start from a blank deck:

- **Copy** a Library or public deck to My Decks and it becomes yours to study. See [Managing decks](managing-decks.md).
- **Add to a deck** in the reader (from a word's **More** panel) or on a verb page opens the deck's workspace with the word or verb already filled in.
- **Create a deck from selection** in your [Vocabulary](../vocabulary.md) makes a new deck from the entries you tick.
- Immersive fills the **From reading**, **Bridge comparisons**, and **Grammar practice** decks as you study. See [Decks & Reviews](../decks.md#what-a-deck-holds).
