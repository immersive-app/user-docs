# Creating decks

Building a deck takes two steps: create the deck, then add cards to it. A deck reviews nothing until it has cards, so the workspace takes you straight from one to the other.

---

## To create a deck

1. Open **Decks** and choose **Create a new deck** (the + button).
2. Name the deck in both languages — reviews can show either side, so give it a title in your target language and one in your own.
3. Optionally pick a **folder** (or create a new one right there) and add **tags**. My Decks is the root — a folder is only needed if you want one; tags group decks across folders.
4. Choose **Save and add cards**.

## Adding cards

The second step of the workspace offers a card type switcher — **Word**, **Sentence**, **Verb**, **Number** — plus a bulk route, **Upload spreadsheet**.

### Words

Type a word and, optionally, its translation — leave the translation blank and Immersive fills it in. Pressing Enter adds the card and clears the field for the next word, so you can enter a list quickly.

### Sentences

Enter sentences one per line. Translations auto-fill; you can edit them in the card list afterwards.

### Verbs

1. Search for verbs, or browse the **Essential** (the first verbs a new learner needs), **Common**, and **All** tiers.
2. Pick the **tenses** you want to practise — a **Show only common tenses** toggle keeps the list to the ones you will actually meet.
3. Choose a **card style**: *One card per person* (je parle, tu parles… reviewed separately) or *All persons on one card* (the full tense table on a single card).

Immersive generates one card per person and tense (or per tense table) automatically.

### Numbers

Enter numbers, comma-separated. Each number becomes a card that you will read, write, hear, or say in words.

## Importing from a spreadsheet

Choose **Upload spreadsheet** to bulk-import word cards:

1. Pick a **CSV or XLSX** file — columns are word and translation, and the translation is optional (missing ones auto-fill and are marked so you can glance over them). A template is available to download.
2. Review the **preview**: rows already in the deck arrive unchecked so you do not create duplicates.
3. Select the rows you want and choose **Import cards**.

## Example sentences on word cards

A word card can carry an example sentence. From the card row you can attach one three ways:

- **From your documents** — sentences you have read that contain the word appear first.
- **Write your own** example sentence.
- **Generate** — Immersive writes one sentence at your level; regenerate if it does not fit.

## Other ways decks get made

You do not always start from a blank deck:

- **Copy** a Library or public deck to My Decks and it becomes yours to study. See [Managing decks](managing-decks.md).
- **Create a deck from selection** in your [Vocabulary](../vocabulary.md).
- **Add comparison card** on a verb conjugation page files cross-language comparison cards into a dedicated deck. See [Working with verbs](../verbs/working-with-verbs.md).
- Revealing hinted words in the reader builds a "From reading" deck as you read. See [Reading documents](../documents/reading-documents.md).
