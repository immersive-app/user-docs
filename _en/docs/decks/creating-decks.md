# Creating decks

A deck you make is one of two kinds: a **verb deck**, made from the verbs and tenses you choose, or a **vocabulary deck**, filled with your own words and sentences. You choose the kind when you create the deck.

## To create a deck

Open **Decks** and choose the **+** button (**Create a new deck**). **New deck** offers the two kinds:

- **Verbs**: "Choose verbs and tenses."
- **Vocabulary**: "Name it, then type or paste its words and sentences, upload a list, or pick them from a text."

## A verb deck

Choosing **Verbs** opens **New verb deck**:

1. Pick verbs from the **Essential**, **Common**, or **All** list. Essential holds the first verbs a new learner needs; Common and All have a search box. Immersive remembers the list you used last.
2. Tick the **Tenses** you want to practise.
3. Check the **Name**. Immersive suggests one in both languages from the tenses you ticked; change it if you like. Optionally pick a **Folder**.
4. The bar at the foot of the page counts the cards ("24 cards", "12 verbs × 2 tenses"). Choose **Create deck**.

Each verb in each tense becomes one card, holding all six forms, each with its audio, and an example sentence for each person. To add or remove verbs and tenses later, edit the deck: see [Managing decks](managing-decks.md#verb-decks).

## A vocabulary deck

Choosing **Vocabulary** opens the first step:

1. Give the deck a title in the language you are learning and one in your own language. Both are required: reviews can show either side.
2. Optionally pick a **Folder** (or type a new folder name) and add **Tags**. Leaving the folder blank keeps the deck at the top level of My Decks; tags group decks across folders.
3. Choose **Create**.

The deck's workspace opens on **Add cards**, with "Deck created."

## Adding cards

A vocabulary deck's workspace has one box, **Type or paste**, and three other ways in under it: **Upload a spreadsheet**, **A document or a photo**, and **Saved while reading**.

### Type or paste

Put one word or one sentence on each line. A line with one word becomes a word card; a line with several words becomes a sentence card. An article or a particle and one word count as a word: *la revanche*, *l'eau*, *se lever*, *to run*.

For your own translation, put it after a tab or after " = ", for example `marché = market`. A list exported from Quizlet already has the tab. A line without a translation gets one automatically.

Under the box, a count shows what saving adds ("Saving adds 5 cards: 3 words, 2 sentences"). Choose **Save**: it also saves any change you made under **Edit deck**. Up to 500 lines are added at a time.

A line that could not be added stays in the box, and the reason is under it: the line has no word in it (a line of "=" alone, or a number), it has no translation while automatic translation is unavailable, or its card could not be saved. Fix the line and choose **Save** again.

### From a spreadsheet

Choose **Upload a spreadsheet** to import many cards at once:

1. Choose a **CSV or XLSX** file with two columns: the text in the language you are learning, and its translation. The translation is optional. **Download template.csv** gives you a file to start from. Up to 500 rows are read.
2. Choose **Preview**. Each row becomes a word card or a sentence card, depending on its text. Rows already in the deck are marked **In Deck** and arrive unticked, so you do not create duplicates; translations Immersive filled in are marked **Auto** so you can glance over them.
3. Tick the rows you want and choose **Import cards**.

### Documents and photos

Choose **A document or a photo** to pick cards out of a text:

1. Choose the text: **Take a photo** (on a phone) or **Upload a photo**, **Upload a document**, or one of your documents under **From your documents**. A photo or file you send becomes a document in your [Documents](../documents.md), and Immersive reads it, which takes a few seconds ("Reading the text…").
2. The text's **Sentences** and **Words** are listed on two tabs, all unticked. Words you already have are left out. Tick what you want, or **Select all**; **Find a word** filters a long list of words.
3. The bar counts your ticks ("7 ticked: 4 sentences, 3 words"). Choose **Add 7 cards**. Up to 500 cards are added at a time.

The cards appear on the deck's page under the text's title.

### Saved while reading

**Saved while reading** opens your [Vocabulary](../vocabulary.md): the words you tap or save while reading, which are already cards in your **My vocabulary** deck.

## Example sentences

A word card can carry an example sentence. In the card list, choose **Add example** (or **Change example**) on the card, then:

- **From your documents**: sentences you have read that contain the word appear first.
- **Write your own**: type a sentence and choose **Attach sentence**.
- **Generate**: Immersive writes one sentence at your level; choose **Regenerate** if it does not fit.

A verb deck's cards come with their example sentences.

## Other ways decks get made

You do not always start from a blank deck:

- **Copy** a Library or public deck to My Decks and it becomes yours to study. See [Managing decks](managing-decks.md).
- **Add to a deck** in the reader (from a word's **More** panel) opens a vocabulary deck's workspace with the word or sentence in the box.
- **Add to a deck** on a verb page opens one of your verb decks with the verb added, or, with no verb deck in that language yet, **New verb deck** with the verb ticked. See [Working with verbs](../verbs/working-with-verbs.md#adding-a-verb-to-a-deck).
- Immersive fills the **My vocabulary**, **From reading**, **Bridge comparisons**, and **Grammar practice** decks as you study. See [Decks & Reviews](../decks.md#two-kinds-of-deck).
