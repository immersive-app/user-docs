# Reviewing cards

A review session shows you the cards that are due, one at a time, full screen. Each answer feeds the schedule: cards you know well move further out, cards you miss come back soon. Reviews use spaced repetition that adapts to your memory (the FSRS schedule).

## Starting a session

Open one of your decks. If cards are waiting, the deck page shows one big button, **Start review** with the number of cards, and a caption splitting that number into due and new cards. Press it, or press Space.

If nothing is due, the deck tells you when the next card comes due. Once the day's new cards are done, it says how many are left, and **Study deck** goes through the whole deck with no daily limit.

A deck with due cards also shows its **Due** count as a link on the Decks page, which starts the review directly.

To review a Library or public deck, copy it to My Decks first.

## The four modes

Tabs at the top of the card switch the mode. Immersive remembers the mode you chose, for all your decks, and starts there next time.

- **Read**: read the prompt and recall the answer in your head, then press **Show answer** (or Space) and grade yourself. The number keys 1 to 4 choose a grade. Once the answer is on the card, its words work as in the reader: tap one for its translation and audio.
- **Write**: the card shows the translation; write the answer in the language you are learning. **Tap** gives you shuffled word tiles to put in order (placing the last word submits), or switch to **Type** and use the keyboard, then **Submit** (or Cmd+Enter / Ctrl+Enter).
- **Listen**: the card is spoken; write what you hear, with the same **Tap** and **Type** choice. If a card has no audio, it shows the text instead.
- **Speak**: read the prompt aloud. **Record** captures your voice and stops by itself after ten seconds. A moment after you submit, a transcript of what you said appears under **You said**, with the words that differ from the answer marked, and the grade it earned ("Close: 92%"). Play back **Yours** and the **Reference** to hear the difference. If your recording cannot be checked, **Try again** records it again.

The Pronunciation deck has none of these four: it is reviewed in **Hear** and **Say**, and its rounds are graded by their own rules. See [Pronunciation](pronunciation.md).

## The verb card

A card in a verb deck is one verb in one tense.

- **Read**: the card shows the verb, its tense, and its meaning. Recall the six forms, then press **Show answer**: the six forms appear, each with its audio, and an example sentence under them. Tap a form, or play it, to see that person's sentence; tap the sentence to see its translation.
- **Write**, **Listen**, and **Speak** ask for one person at a time, in that person's example sentence. In Write and Listen the form is left blank, and Listen plays the sentence and hides its translation. In Speak you read the whole sentence aloud. The line under the sentence names the verb, the person, and the tense ("avoir · je · Indicatif · Futur simple"); in Listen it leaves out the person. Tap the line, or press P, to move to the next person without answering.
- In **Write** and **Listen**, **Tap** offers whole forms to choose from; one tap answers. **Type** takes the form with or without its pronoun ("j'aurai" or "aurai"); Enter sends it.

A written form is right or wrong. The result shows the whole sentence with the form marked, your answer, **Correct** or **Incorrect**, the right form after a wrong one, and when the card comes back. A missing or wrong accent counts as wrong, and the result says **Missing accent**. Capitals, spaces, and the kind of apostrophe make no difference.

Immersive picks the person each time: one you have not yet answered right comes first. One schedule covers the whole card, whichever person you were asked.

## Grading

Every review ends in one of four grades:

- **Again**: you did not recall it; the card returns very soon.
- **Hard**: you got there, with difficulty.
- **Good**: recalled with some effort.
- **Easy**: effortless; the card moves furthest out.

In Read mode you choose the grade, and each grade button shows when it brings the card back ("1 minute", "1 day", "7 days"). In Write and Listen, Immersive grades your answer itself. The result shows **Correct** or **Incorrect**; after a wrong answer, the right one ("Answer · j'ai"); the reason where there is one, such as **Missing accent** or a question's explanation; and when the card comes back ("Back in 1 minute", "Back tomorrow"). A verb form is right or wrong: right is **Easy**, wrong is **Again**. Other answers are graded by how close they are, so a small slip can still be right, with the answer shown under it. In Speak, Immersive grades a transcript of what you said. **Next** saves the review and moves on.

## Skip

**Skip** sits at the foot of the answer in Write, Listen, and Speak, beside **Submit** or **Record**, and under the card in a Pronunciation round. A skipped card is not graded and its schedule does not change: it leaves this session and comes back when you next press **Start review**. Read has no Skip. On a computer, the S key skips.

## The card menu

The button at the top left of every card (**Card actions**) opens its menu:

- **Suspend card** takes the card out of your reviews until you bring it back. The next card shows **Card suspended · Undo**, so you can change your mind; later, bring it back from the foot of the deck's page. See [Managing decks](managing-decks.md#suspended-cards).
- **Edit card**, on a word or sentence card in your own deck, opens the card to change its text, translation, or example, then returns you to the review.
- **Report this sentence**, on a verb card in Read once the answer is shown, sends the sentence to be looked at, with an optional note. Choose **Send report**.

## Keyboard

On a computer:

- **Space** shows the answer in Read; **1** to **4** choose a grade.
- **S** skips the card.
- **P** moves a verb card to the next person.
- **Cmd+Enter** (Ctrl+Enter) submits a typed answer; a typed verb form is sent with Enter.

## During the session

- A thin progress bar tracks the session, with your place beside it, such as "3 of 12". A card you answer wrong comes back later in the session, so it does not move your place on.
- A line under the card counts what is left: due today, new, and learning.
- Once the answer is shown, **Ask about this card** opens the [Coach](../coach.md) with the card in view.
- The X at the top of the card returns to the deck.

## Results

When the queue is finished you see **Session complete**: the cards reviewed, how many you recalled first try, and your focused effort time, then each card with when it comes back. Cards you graded **Again** are marked **to revisit**. **Back to deck** returns to the deck.

## Daily limits

Each deck introduces 20 new cards a day. **Settings**, under the deck's **Start review**, changes the number to 5, 10, 20, 40 or 80. There is no limit on reviews of cards you have already started, and they come before new cards. A card you answer wrong comes back the same day. When the day's new cards are done, the session goes on with the cards that are due, then ends by saying how many new cards are left, with **Study deck** and **Settings**.

## Review history

On the Decks page, the clock opens your **Review history**: every session, newest first and grouped by day, with the deck, the mode, the number of cards, how many you recalled, and how long it took. When a session had cards graded Again or Hard, **to revisit** starts a short session with just those cards, in the same mode. Revisits are real reviews: they update the schedule like any other.

## The daily review email

When cards are waiting, Immersive can email you each morning. See [Navigation](../dashboard.md#the-daily-review-email).
