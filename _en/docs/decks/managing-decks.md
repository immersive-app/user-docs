# Managing decks

The Decks page is your library of study material. This page covers keeping it organised: reading the deck list, copying ready-made decks, a deck's page, editing, suspended cards, folders, tags, bookmarks, exporting to Anki, and deleting what you no longer need.

## The deck list

**My Decks** lists your decks with three columns:

- **New**: cards you have not seen yet.
- **Learn**: cards you are in the middle of learning.
- **Due**: cards whose review time has arrived. A due count is a link that starts the review.

These numbers tell you at a glance where your review time is needed.

## The Library

Above your decks sits the **Library**: ready-made decks from Immersive in the language you are learning, with a count of how many there are. It starts folded; open it to see the decks. It includes most-common-words decks split by frequency (the first 500 words, then 501 to 1,000, then 1,001 to 5,000), so you never study the same word twice, and verb decks, **Essential verbs** and **Common verbs**, one for each tense, with a card for each verb's whole tense. For all but two pairs of languages, the first deck in the list is **Pronunciation**, for the sounds your own language does not tell apart; see [Pronunciation](pronunciation.md).

Library decks are studied from your own copy: press the **+** next to one (**Copy to My Decks**) and the copy appears in My Decks.

## Public decks

Verb pages and verb form pages list **Public decks** that practise that verb. Open one to see its cards, and choose **Copy to My Decks** to study it. Your copy shows which deck it was copied from.

## A deck's page

Open a deck to see its page: **Start review** (see [Reviewing cards](reviewing-cards.md)), the deck's actions (**Study deck**, **Export to Anki**, and **Copy to My Decks** on a Library or public deck), and its cards.

- **A verb deck** lists a section for each tense, with a row for each verb: its meaning, and **Study** to study that card alone. Open a verb to see its card: the six forms with their audio, then **Sentences**, each person's example sentence with its translation and audio.
- **A vocabulary deck** lists its cards, 25 at a time. Cards you picked from a text are grouped under the text's title.

## Folders

Folders keep a long deck list manageable:

- **Create** a folder while creating or editing a deck: pick an existing folder or type a new folder name.
- **Move** a deck with **Move to folder** in the deck's menu on the Decks page.
- A folder's menu lets you **rename** it, start a **New deck in folder**, or **Delete folder**. Deleting a folder never deletes decks: they return to My Decks.

Folders open and close in place on the Decks page.

## Tags

Tags group decks across folders. Add them when creating or editing a deck; tapping a tag on a deck page filters the Decks list to decks with that tag.

## Bookmarks

The bookmark icon on a deck (in the list, or on the deck page) marks it. The bookmark button at the top of the Decks page then shows only your bookmarked decks.

## Editing a deck

Open a deck and choose the pencil to open its workspace. **Edit deck** opens the settings: rename it in either language, change its folder, or adjust its tags. The workspace has one **Save** (in the app, at the top of the screen), which saves the settings together with the box on a vocabulary deck or the table on a verb deck. If either cannot be saved (a title left empty, for example), nothing is saved and your changes stay on the page.

### Vocabulary decks

Under the settings are **Add cards** (see [Creating decks](creating-decks.md#adding-cards)) and the card list, which lets you edit or delete individual cards and add examples. It shows 25 cards at a time, and **Show more** lists the rest.

### Verb decks

A verb deck's workspace is one table, **Verbs and tenses**: verbs down, tenses across, and a tick where the deck has that card. On a phone it is a list, each verb with its tenses as buttons to turn on and off.

- **Add verbs** opens the verb picker (**Essential**, **Common**, **All**, with a search). Each verb you add is a new row, marked **New**, ticked in every tense.
- **Add a tense** lists the tenses the deck does not have yet, and adds a column ticked for every verb.
- **Remove** on a verb, or **Remove this tense** in a tense's menu, takes it out; **Undo** puts it back.
- Untick a box to remove one card, tick it to add one. **Find a verb** filters a long table.

Nothing changes until you choose **Save**. A line beside it says what Save will do, for example "24 cards. Saving adds 3 and removes 1." A card you remove after studying it keeps its review history: add it again and it comes back where you left it. A verb deck keeps at least one card; to remove them all, delete the deck.

## Suspended cards

A card you suspend during a review (see [Reviewing cards](reviewing-cards.md#the-card-menu)) leaves your reviews until you bring it back. At the foot of the deck's page, a line counts them ("3 suspended"). Open it to list them, and choose **Bring back** on a card, or **Bring all back**. A card brought back keeps its schedule and its history.

## Exporting to Anki

**Export to Anki**, among a deck's actions on a computer, prepares an Anki file of the whole deck in the background and emails it to you when it is ready. It works for your decks, Library decks, and public ones:

- **A verb deck**: a note for each verb and tense, with its forms.
- **A vocabulary deck**: a note for each word or sentence, with its translation, its audio, and the card's example sentence when it has one.

Suspended cards are left out, and so are cards of other kinds, such as grammar cards; the email says how many. A deck with nothing to export has no export button.

## Deleting a deck

Delete a deck with **Delete deck**, at the foot of its workspace. Deleting removes the deck, its cards, and their review progress; you are asked to confirm first.
