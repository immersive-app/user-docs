# Managing decks

The Decks page is your library of study material. This page covers keeping it organised: reading the deck list, copying ready-made decks, folders, tags, bookmarks, editing, exporting to Anki, and deleting what you no longer need.

## The deck list

**My Decks** lists your decks with three columns:

- **New**: cards you have not seen yet.
- **Learn**: cards you are in the middle of learning.
- **Due**: cards whose review time has arrived. A due count is a link that starts the review.

These numbers tell you at a glance where your review time is needed.

## The Library

Above your decks sits the **Library**: ready-made decks from Immersive in the language you are learning, with a count of how many there are. It starts folded; open it to see the decks. It includes most-common-words decks split by frequency (the first 500 words, then 501 to 1,000, then 1,001 to 5,000), so you never study the same word twice, and for some languages an **Essentials** deck of core verbs in the present tense.

Library decks are studied from your own copy: press the **+** next to one (**Copy to My Decks**) and the copy appears in My Decks.

## Public decks

Verb pages and verb form pages list **Public decks** that practise that verb. Open one to see its cards, and choose **Copy to My Decks** to study it. Your copy shows which deck it was copied from.

## Folders

Folders keep a long deck list manageable:

- **Create** a folder while creating or editing a deck: pick an existing folder or type a new folder name.
- **Move** a deck with **Move to folder** in the deck's menu on the Decks page.
- A folder's menu lets you **rename** it, start a **New deck in folder**, or **Delete folder**. Deleting a folder never deletes decks: they return to My Decks.

Folders open and close in place on the Decks page.

## Tags

Tags group decks across folders. Add them when creating or editing a deck; tapping a tag on a deck page filters the Decks list to decks with that tag.

## Bookmarks

The bookmark icon on a deck (in the list, or on the deck page) marks it. The bookmark button at the top of the Decks page then shows only your bookmarked decks.

## Editing a deck

Open a deck and choose the pencil to open its workspace. **Edit deck** opens the settings: rename it in either language, change its folder, or adjust its tags, then choose **Update Deck**. The card list below lets you edit or delete individual cards and add examples.

## Exporting to Anki

**Export to Anki** on one of your decks prepares an Anki file in the background and emails it to you when it is ready. To export a Library or public deck, copy it to My Decks first.

## Deleting a deck

Delete a deck with the trash button in its workspace. Deleting removes the deck, its cards, and their review progress; you are asked to confirm first.
