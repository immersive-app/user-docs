# Books

A book is a long text in chapters: a novel, a collection of stories, a textbook. You read it one chapter at a time, in the same reader as any document, with the same tap-to-translate, aids, and **Read to me**. Immersive keeps your place, so you open the book where you left it.

Books come first on the **Documents** page, above your documents and the **Library**. A book's chapters are not listed among your documents: you reach them through the book. A book is private: only you see it.

## Adding a book

1. On **Documents**, choose **Add a book** in the **Books** section. On the web, the **+** button offers it too.
2. **Upload a file** (**Choose a file**, or drop one on the box: EPUB, text, Markdown, or PDF, up to 25 MB), or **Paste a Project Gutenberg link**. Use one or the other, not both.
3. **Title** and **Author** are optional. Left blank, they come from the file.
4. **Add book**. The book's page opens.

The form reminds you: **Import only what you have the right to read.**

The chapters come from the file: an EPUB's table of contents, a Markdown file's headings, lines such as "Chapter 1" in a text file, or a PDF's outline. A chapter over 10,000 words is cut into parts, and a text with no headings becomes "Part 1", "Part 2", and so on. You can change the chapters later (see [Editing the contents](#editing-the-contents)). A PDF that holds pictures of pages, such as a scan, cannot be read as a book.

A book is added in the language you are learning. A book in another language still imports, with a warning on its page; **Dismiss** puts the warning away.

### Project Gutenberg

[Project Gutenberg](https://www.gutenberg.org) is a library of free ebooks. Paste a link to one of its books (the book's page or its EPUB link) and Immersive fetches it for you, with its title, author, and cover. The book's page names the ebook and its licence, the Project Gutenberg License.

You can add one book from a link at a time, and up to ten in 24 hours. If the fetch fails, the book's page says why and offers **Try again** or **Upload the EPUB**: download the EPUB from the book's page on gutenberg.org and upload it there. Uploading a file has no such limit.

## Importing

The book's page shows the import as it goes: first it reads the file, then it prepares the chapters ("Preparing chapter 4 of 24"). You can start reading the chapters that are ready; the others say **Waiting**. On the **Documents** page the book shows **Importing…** until it is done.

If the import fails, the page says why. **Try again** imports the same file from the start.

## Reading

On the **Documents** page, a book's row shows how far you are, with **Start** for a new book and **Continue** for one you are reading. Tap the row for the book's page: the cover, the contents, and the way in, **Read chapter 1** or **Continue reading**.

A chapter opens in the reader:

- The bar at the top shows the book's title and the chapter ("Chapter 2 of 24 · ..."); tap it for the book's page. The close button goes back to the book's page too.
- A thin line under the bar fills as you read through the chapter.
- In the reading controls (the sliders button), the first row is **Contents**. The arrows open the previous and next chapter. The middle ("2 of 24") opens the list of chapters: the one you are in says **Reading**, the ones you have read are ticked, and **Book page** opens the book's page. Pick a chapter to open it.
- At the bottom of the reading controls you see how far you are: the share of the chapter, the minutes left in it, and the share of the book.
- On a computer, the left and right arrow keys open the previous and next chapter.

## End of chapter

After the last paragraph comes the end of the chapter, with the next chapter's title, length, and reading time. **Next chapter** opens it, and **Back to the book** returns to the book's page. Reaching the end marks the chapter as read. **Read to me** stops at the end of a chapter.

After the last chapter, the book is finished.

## Your place

Immersive keeps your place as you read, down to the sentence, and on every device you use. **Continue** on the book's row, or **Continue reading** on its page, opens the chapter you were in at the sentence you left.

The book's page shows how much of the book you have read and about how long is left. A finished book shows **Finished**, and **Read again** starts at chapter 1 and keeps your ticks.

## Editing the contents

If a book was cut in the wrong places, choose **Edit** beside **Contents** on the book's page, once every chapter is ready. Each change is saved as you make it. On a phone, each chapter's ⋯ button opens its actions.

- **Rename**: type a new title in place. Enter, or leaving the field, saves it; Escape puts the old one back.
- **Move up** and **Move down** change the order.
- **Merge into “…”** joins a chapter to the one before it. Every word is kept, and the merged chapter's title stays in the text where it started.
- **Split…** cuts a chapter in two. Choose where the new chapter starts, at a heading or a paragraph, then **Split here**. Every word is kept.
- **Remove — deletes the text** deletes the chapter and its text: use it for front and back matter you will not read. A book keeps at least one chapter.
- **Chapters are split at** shows where the book is cut now, when it could be cut another way. Pick another place (**Heading 1** to **Heading 3**, or for a text file **Chapter lines**, **Numerals**, or **Capitals**), then **Split again**: the whole book is cut again from the text as it was imported. Renamed chapters and your reading position are kept where the text still matches.

Your place follows its sentence when chapters are merged or split. If you remove the chapter you are in, your place moves to the next one.

**Done** returns to the book's page. **Cancel** leaves without saving a title you are still typing.

## Deleting a book

**Delete book**, at the foot of the book's page, deletes the book and all its chapters once you confirm. This cannot be undone.
