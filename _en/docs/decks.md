# Decks & Reviews

Decks are where memorising happens in Immersive. A deck is a collection of cards, and reviewing a deck shows you each card at the moment you are about to forget it, using spaced repetition that adapts to your memory (the FSRS schedule, the same family of scheduling used by modern Anki).

## Two kinds of deck

A deck you make is one of two kinds:

- **Verb decks**: you choose verbs and tenses, and Immersive makes one card for each verb in each tense. A card holds all six forms of that tense, each with its audio and an example sentence.
- **Vocabulary decks**: your own words and sentences, one card each. You type or paste them, upload a spreadsheet, pick them from a document or a photo, or save them while reading.

A deck holds one kind of card, never both. See [Creating decks](decks/creating-decks.md).

Immersive also fills some decks for you as you study. They appear in My Decks like any other:

- **My vocabulary**: a card for each word you tap while reading, and each word or sentence you save. See [Vocabulary](vocabulary.md).
- **From reading (language)**: a card each time you reveal a guessable word in the reader. See [Reading documents](documents/reading-documents.md).
- **Grammar practice (language)**: cards from the grammar notes you practise. See [Grammar practice](grammar/grammar-practice.md).

The Library holds ready-made decks of both kinds, and the **Pronunciation** deck. See [Managing decks](decks/managing-decks.md#the-library).

## How reviewing works

Each card is scheduled on its own. When you review a card, it is graded **Again**, **Hard**, **Good**, or **Easy**, and the schedule adjusts: cards you find easy move further into the future, cards you miss come back quickly. Over time your review minutes go to the material that needs them.

You can review in four modes (**Read**, **Write**, **Listen**, and **Speak**), so the same deck trains recognition, spelling, listening, and speaking. A card you do not want to answer now can be skipped, and one you do not want to study at all can be suspended. See [Reviewing cards](decks/reviewing-cards.md). The ready-made **Pronunciation** deck has two modes of its own, **Hear** and **Say**. See [Pronunciation](decks/pronunciation.md).

## The Decks page

Opening **Decks** shows:

- **Library**: ready-made decks in the language you are learning, folded closed until you open it. Copy one to My Decks with its **+** button.
- **My Decks**: your decks and folders, with columns showing how many cards are **New**, in **Learn**ing, and **Due**.
- Three buttons at the top: the clock opens your **Review history**, the bookmark shows only your bookmarked decks, and **+** creates a new deck.

## Anki

If you also use Anki:

- **Export to Anki** on a deck's page, on a computer, prepares an Anki file (.apkg) of the whole deck and emails it to you. It works for verb decks and vocabulary decks, yours or one from the Library. See [Managing decks](decks/managing-decks.md#exporting-to-anki).
- The **Anki** page (linked in the footer of Immersive's public pages) explains the export, with **Go to my decks** when you are signed in. When ready-made decks are available, it lists them under **Free decks to download**: signed in, you download directly; without an account, you enter your email address to download, and Immersive also sends you its free Bridge Method email course, five short lessons, which you can unsubscribe from at any time. The course stops when you create an account with that address.
