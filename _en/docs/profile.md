# Profile & Progress

Your Profile is the command centre for everything about you as a learner: what to do next, where you stand, what you have done, the words you have collected, and your account itself. Open it with **Home**, the last tab of the navigation, which opens on its Home tab. The tabs are **Home**, **Activity**, **Evaluate**, **Vocabulary**, and **Account**.

---

## Home

Home puts the one thing to do now at the top, then what is waiting, then where you stand.

- **At the top**, the next step. With cards due in your decks, it counts them ("3 cards are due"), names the deck with the most, and offers **Start review**. With nothing due, it says **All caught up**, and **Continue** opens the grammar note to carry on with; while you are new, it says "Start at B1" ("Start from A1" until you set a level), and **Start** opens the first note. Due grammar exercises are not counted here: **Continue** leads to the note they are on. Under it, your level ("Level **B1**, set by you"), with **Change**.
- **Set your level**, until you have one: tap the level that sounds like you, or **Take the level test** where the language has one (see [Level test](#level-test)). A test left unfinished is offered again, with **Resume the test**.
- **Reading**: the book or text you are reading (**Continue reading**), and the next Library text at your level, with **Start**. With neither, **Browse** leads to the Library.
- **Grammar by level**: each level with your mastery and its due grammar cards, and each of its areas shaded by your mastery. On a phone, tap a level to show its areas, with **Open B1** and **Test yourself on B1**; on a wider screen, the areas are all shown, and the button with a tick beside a level's mastery is its quiz (see [Test yourself](grammar/grammar-practice.md#test-yourself)).
- **Decks due**: up to five decks with cards due, most first, and **All decks**. With none due, it says **Nothing due in your decks**; with no deck yet, **Start with a ready-made deck** leads to the Library.
- **This week**, once you have cards, documents, or vocabulary: the days you studied in the last seven, and the week's sessions, time studied, and reviews. A count, never a streak: a day off loses nothing.

**Where you stand**, likewise once you have begun. Immersive tracks **comprehension** and **production** separately, because understanding legitimately runs ahead of speaking and writing — that gap is the method working, not a problem. **Comprehension** shows the level you gave yourself, with the evidence gathering under it for Reading and Listening. **Production** shows the middle grade of your corrected writing, with the evidence for Writing and Speaking (graded writing, evaluations from classmates).

**Languages.** The language you are learning first, with its level (**Level not set** until you choose one), then the languages you speak, with **Edit** to change them. Comparisons and hints use these.

### Level test

The level test finds your level in the language you are learning, so you do not have to guess it. It is offered where that language's grammar notes have exercises enough: on Home, under **Set your level**, until you have a level, and on the language grid of your first sign-in (**Not sure of your level? Take the level test**).

- It starts at A1 and asks up to five questions a level, one at a time, from that level's grammar notes. They are your own grammar cards, made in **Grammar practice** as a quiz makes them (see [Test yourself](grammar/grammar-practice.md#test-yourself)): pick an option or type the answer, then **Check**. **Skip** passes over a question you do not know.
- A level is passed when you answer at least three of its questions and at least three in five of your answers are right. Then the next level starts, up to C1, so the questions get harder while you keep up; a level not passed ends the test. At most it asks 25 questions.
- **Finish the test** ends it whenever you have reached your level.
- Each answer is saved as you give it, as a review of the question's card, so you can stop and come back: Home offers **Resume the test**.

The result, **Your level**, shows the level you are placed at: the last level you passed, counting up from A1, or A1 if you passed none. It is saved straight away as your level in that language, which Home and the Grammar page start from. **By level** lists each level asked, with how many you got right and how many you answered, **Passed** or **Not yet**. Every question you answered is now a card in your decks and comes back for review like any other.

Home offers the test only while your level is not set. Once it is, **Change** beside your level sets another one by hand.

## Activity

Your study, added up — effort only, never judgement:

- **This week** and **All time**: sessions, time studied, and reviews.
- **By domain**: a row for each area — your decks (cards mastered, learning, new, and due today), your grammar (working level, notes started, notes fluent, and cards due now), your documents, your vocabulary, and your graded writing. The rows for decks, grammar, documents, and vocabulary open their section.
- A **heatmap** of the last 12 months: one square per day, darker meaning more study, with your total study days, longest run, and current run.
- The **study log**: your sessions and reviews, day by day, filterable by kind of activity, with a full log available.

## Evaluate

After studying with a classmate, you can trade quick evaluations — one overall impression (Excellent, Good, or Getting there) plus an optional comment. It is a courtesy between learners, never a ranking.

- **Show my code** shows your code as a QR code, with the code itself underneath. Your classmate scans the QR code with their phone's camera, or types the code. Codes last 10 minutes and work once.
- To evaluate a classmate, scan the QR code on their screen with your phone's camera, or choose **Enter a code** and type it. Then pick an impression, add a sentence if you like, and send.

Evaluations you give and receive are listed on the tab, and spoken evaluations count as evidence under **Where you stand** on Home.

## Vocabulary

The words and sentences you have collected while studying, searchable and filterable. The words you tap and the entries you save are cards in your **My vocabulary** deck, with **Study** at the top of the tab. See [Vocabulary](vocabulary.md).

## Account

Everything administrative lives here:

- **Account** — first name, last name, and **How we address you**. Your email address is shown here.
  How we address you is optional: **Not indicated** (the default), **Feminine**, or **Masculine**. Some languages change words for the person they are about, like French *prêt* and *prête*; it sets the form used for you.
- **Profile** — location and profile picture.
- **Languages** — a tile for each of the six languages with your level; tap one to say whether you speak it or are learning it, and at what level. Under the grid, **Learning language** and **Display language** are the pair the navigation bar shows, and a switch hides that button if you prefer. **Use the languages I know to explain the one I'm learning** turns on the cross-language comparisons: the *Across your languages* box on grammar notes and verb forms, the six-language equivalents in the reader, and the [grammar of reading](grammar/grammar-of-reading.md) with its dotted underlines. It is off by default — most people learn one language at a time. Which variety of each language the interface uses is on [Language varieties](language-varieties.md).
- **Voices**, on a premium account only — **Premium voices** plays ElevenLabs voices on sentences, words, and verb forms instead of the usual ones. A clip is made the first time you play it; until then you hear the usual voice. Turning it on makes a premium voice the reader's **Reading voice** in each language that has one, and you can still pick another there; turning it off goes back to the usual voices.
- **Emails** — two emails, each with its switch and **Save**:
  - **Daily review email**, on unless you turn it off: what is due in your decks, each morning. See [Navigation](dashboard.md#the-daily-review-email).
  - **Weekly workout email**, off until you turn it on: every Monday at about 8:00 in your time zone, the number of cards due across your decks with **Open Home**, and **A topic to write about** at your level, in the language you are learning; **Start writing** opens a new document with the topic filled in. **Stop receiving this weekly email**, at the bottom of each one, turns it off, as does the switch here.
- **Theme** — the look of Immersive: **Classic**, **Paper**, **Quiet**, or **Solarized**, each with an **Appearance** of **Light**, **Dark**, or **System** (follows your device). Saved as you pick. The reader's controls offer the same choice.
- **Sign-in methods** — whether your account is linked to Sign in with Apple or Google, with **Connect** and **Disconnect**. See [Your account](account.md#signing-in).
- **What the app may do**, in the iOS app only — what the phone lets Immersive use: the **Camera** (to photograph a page to import, take a profile picture, or scan a classmate's code) and **Notifications**. Each says **On**, **Off**, or **Not asked yet**. **Allow camera** and **Allow notifications** ask the phone; once you have said no, only the phone's Settings can change it, and the button becomes **Open Settings**. The microphone is not listed: the phone asks for it the first time you record yourself, in Speak mode for example, and you change it in the phone's Settings.
- **Password** — changing it asks for your current password. Your email address is set at sign-up and cannot be changed in the app; use the contact form if you need it changed.
- **Your data** — **Download your personal data** emails you a link to everything we hold about you: your
  decks and cards, your documents and the files you uploaded to make them, your writing, your chats with the
  coach, your vocabulary, your review history and the record of what you did in the app. It arrives as a zip
  file. You have to be signed in to download it, and the link works for a week.
  On a premium account, **Keep my AI requests for review** is here too, on unless you turn it off: when it is on, the text you send to the AI and its replies are kept so the team can check and improve the answers; when it is off, only the counts are kept.
- **Remove my account** — deletes your account and all its data. This cannot be undone.

In the iOS app, the foot of the tab also has **Privacy policy**, **Terms of Service**, **Contact**, and **Log out**.
